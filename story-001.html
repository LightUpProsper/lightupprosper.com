Where It All Started

The backyard project that sparked the idea for Light Up Prosper and introduced our families to the impact of thoughtful landscape lighting.

← Back to Featured Projects

The Story

This backyard already had a beautiful pool, spa, mature landscaping, and several places to gather. After sunset, however, much of the landscape disappeared into darkness.

The goal was not to overpower the pool. It was to add warmth and depth around it—highlighting palms, trees, the waterfall, and the outdoor living areas while keeping the space relaxed.

That first project became a hands-on introduction to landscape lighting and helped inspire the students to launch Light Up Prosper.

Every lighting plan is carefully designed before installation.

Project Highlights

  • Pool and outdoor-living lighting
  • Layered tree and palm uplighting
  • Waterfall and landscape accents
  • Warm, low-glare atmosphere
