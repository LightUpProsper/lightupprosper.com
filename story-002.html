The Story

Our home became a place to learn what looks good on paper and what actually works after sunset.

We experimented with fixture placement, beam spread, brightness, tree lighting, entry lighting, and the final nighttime aiming process. Some lights moved more than once before the design felt balanced.

That willingness to test and refine still guides every project we complete today.

Every lighting plan is carefully designed before installation.

Project Highlights

  • Warm front-elevation lighting
  • Mature oak uplighting
  • Entry and walkway visibility
  • Nighttime aiming and adjustments
