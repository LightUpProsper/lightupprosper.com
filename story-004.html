One Tree Changes Everything

A mature shade tree becomes the focal point of an entire front yard.

← Back to Featured Projects

The Story

Every property has something worth celebrating. On this home, the large mature tree already framed the yard during the day, but disappeared after sunset.

We built the lighting plan around that tree, then used warm architectural lighting across the white brick to create depth and guide attention toward the entry.

The finished design is simple and balanced. The home feels more welcoming, while the tree gives the whole property a stronger sense of arrival.

Every lighting plan is carefully designed before installation.

Project Highlights

  • Mature-tree focal lighting
  • Warm white-brick uplighting
  • Balanced front-elevation lighting
  • Entry and curb-appeal enhancement
