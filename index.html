Why Light Up Prosper

Built by Students. Backed by Family.

Light Up Prosper was founded by local students who wanted to build something they could be proud of while serving their community.

The students help lead the consultation, planning, installation, and final nighttime adjustments. Families, mentors, and supplier partners provide support, but the effort and pride belong to the student team.

Our approach: honest recommendations, thoughtful design, and treating every home like it is our own.
